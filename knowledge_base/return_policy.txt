COMPANY RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

1. Return Window:
Customers can return eligible products within 30 days
of receiving their order.

2. Product Condition:
Products must be unused and returned in their original
packaging.

3. Refund Processing:
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
after the returned product passes inspection.

4. Non-Returnable Products:
Personalized products and items marked as non-returnable
cannot be returned.

5. Return Shipping:
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs
unless the product was damaged or incorrect.
